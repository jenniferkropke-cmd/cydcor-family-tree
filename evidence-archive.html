HOW TO READ THE ARCHIVE

Evidence packet ≠ proven lineage.

Corporate filings establish legal identities, dates, officers, and addresses. Archived posts can establish employment or management roles. Address overlap can corroborate a handoff. Filing-service overlap is a discovery clue. Each packet explains what the source proves and what it does not.
