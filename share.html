COMMUNITY SUBMISSIONS

Share what you experienced.

Firsthand accounts can add context, reveal missing branches, or correct the public record. Submissions are not published automatically.

Before you submit

Please share only information you are comfortable providing. Do not submit Social Security numbers, banking details, passwords, private medical information, or other highly sensitive personal data.

Moderation rule: A submission is treated as a firsthand account unless supporting documentation independently verifies the claim. Allegations about identifiable people should not be published as fact without corroboration.

Anonymous or pseudonymous submissions are welcome. If you provide contact information, it should be used only to follow up unless you explicitly consent to publication.
