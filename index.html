WHAT THE PROJECT FOUND

It is less a single corporate tree than a reproducible operating pattern.

People are often the genealogy.

Career sequences, promotions, “assistant manager” roles, office launches, and later founder/president positions frequently reveal connections that corporate filings alone do not.

Addresses expose launch ecosystems.

Exact-suite reuse becomes powerful when paired with timing and independently documented personnel movement. Address similarity alone is never treated as proof.

Short-lived offices matter.

“Fizzles” can be structurally important even when they last only 1–3 years because they can sit between training offices and later branches.

Filing infrastructure is not mentorship.

Repeated incorporators and filing services are tracked separately from ownership genealogy to avoid turning administrative overlap into a false family-tree connection.

EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE

How links are graded

CONFIRMED

First-party, corporate/regulatory, or independently corroborated evidence sufficient for the specific link stated.

VERY STRONG / NEAR-CONFIRMED

Multiple strong pieces align, but one ideal first-party person or promotion artifact is still missing.

STRONG

Meaningful personnel, chronology, address, or archive evidence supports the relationship, but not enough for a confirmed arrow.

PROBABLE / RESEARCH LEAD

Plausible, evidence-based direction that should remain dashed or explicitly qualified.

OPEN / UNPLACED

Relevant company or person, but the immediate parent or relationship is not defensibly established.

IDENTITY CONTINUITY

Two labels appear to represent the same operating identity or a successor/rebrand. This is not parent-child genealogy.
